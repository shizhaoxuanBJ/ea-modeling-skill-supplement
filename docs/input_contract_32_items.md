# Input Contract Used in the Study (32 items)

Business departments submitted documents according to this single checklist, shared by all domains (Sect. 5 of the paper, "Input Parser"); they did not draw any views.
Generation order: business architecture first; the data, application and technology architectures take the business-architecture output as input. General materials may serve as input for any of the four architectures.
Counts: business architecture 8, data architecture 7, application architecture 6, technology architecture 3, general materials 8.

| ID | Category | Material | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Business | Organization structure | Organization charts of headquarters and subsidiaries, for identifying stakeholders and roles |
| C02 | Business | Departmental responsibilities | Departmental responsibility documents |
| C03 | Business | Regulations and processes | Management regulations, business processes and sample forms/ledgers, for identifying business objects |
| C04 | Business | Control model | How the group controls projects, for identifying strategic-control capabilities |
| C05 | Business | Business model | Operating model of the main business (e.g. project management model), for value-chain analysis |
| C06 | Business | Business blueprint / value-chain stages | Overall business blueprint, for aligning the stages of the execution layer |
| C07 | Business | Business interview records | Records/minutes of interviews with business departments; primary evidence for capability analysis |
| C08 | Business | Stakeholder information | List of stakeholders per business line, for organizing interviews and confirming results |
| C09 | Data | Data dictionaries (databases and tables, ideally with metadata) | Data dictionaries/table lists of each system, for identifying data entities |
| C10 | Data | Data integration and flows | Producing systems, consuming systems and data flows |
| C11 | Data | System deployment and usage | Which departments use which system and enter which data, for the data distribution view |
| C12 | Data | Master data status | Coding rules and maintaining systems of organization, personnel, customer/supplier, contract, project and material master data; basis for designating authoritative sources |
| C13 | Data | Data standards / business glossary | Current data standards and term definitions; basis for uniform object names |
| C14 | Data | Feedback on duplicate reporting | Concrete cases of duplicate entry and multiple reporting at operational level, for identifying data-governance problems |
| C15 | Data | Reporting rules and report system | Rules and lists for daily, weekly, monthly and statistical reports, for analysing data distribution |

| C21 | Application | IT requirements list | IT requirements raised by business departments |
| C22 | Technology | Platform and infrastructure inventory | Servers, cloud resources, networks, etc. |
| C23 | Technology | Technology stacks and components | Technology stacks and middleware of each system |
| C24 | Technology | Previous IT plans | IT plans of previous years, for continuity and gap analysis |
| C25 | General | Corporate strategy | Group strategic plan |
